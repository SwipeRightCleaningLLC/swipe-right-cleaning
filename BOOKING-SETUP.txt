SWIPE RIGHT CLEANING LLC — GOOGLE CALENDAR WALKTHROUGH SETUP
============================================================

The website now includes a dedicated booking page at:

  booking.html

The page is ready to display your Google Calendar appointment schedule, but the
appointment schedule itself must be created inside your Google Calendar account.

RECOMMENDED APPOINTMENT SETTINGS
--------------------------------
Title:
  Free Commercial Cleaning Walkthrough

Duration:
  30 minutes

Suggested availability:
  - Weekday evenings
  - Saturday mornings
  - Add limited earlier daytime openings only when you are available for select services

Buffer time:
  30 minutes for driving and notes between appointments

Location:
  In-person meeting

Description to paste:
  Book a free on-site walkthrough with Swipe Right Cleaning LLC. We will review
  your facility, discuss the cleaning services and schedule you need, and gather
  the information required to prepare a customized proposal. Please do not enter
  alarm codes, keys, financial information, or other sensitive access details in
  the public booking form.

BOOKING FORM QUESTIONS
----------------------
Google already requires first name, last name, and email. Add these questions:

  1. Business name — required
  2. Facility address — required
  3. Phone number — required
  4. Exact or estimated square footage — required
  5. Type of cleaning needed — required
  6. Access or parking instructions — optional

You may also turn on email verification to reduce spam bookings.

CREATE THE APPOINTMENT SCHEDULE
-------------------------------
1. On a computer, open Google Calendar with your business account.
2. Click Create.
3. Select Appointment schedule.
4. Enter the title, duration, availability, buffer time, location, description,
   and booking-form questions shown above.
5. Save the schedule.

CONNECT IT TO THE WEBSITE
-------------------------
1. In Google Calendar, find the schedule under Booking pages.
2. Hover over it and choose Options.
3. Choose Sharing options, then Website embed.
4. Choose A single booking page.
5. Choose Inline booking page.
6. Click Copy code.
7. In the copied code, find the URL inside src="...".
8. Open script.js in Notepad.
9. Find this line at the top:

   const GOOGLE_CALENDAR_BOOKING_URL = "https://calendar.google.com/appointments/schedules/AcZssZ1fMzj4XxUF-mb0xfKrXAucb7ewb6Y3YXQknHpyF_KqLURYlcZSNXCy7s2oix2Cxd81KK6mp7sA";

10. Paste the URL between the quotation marks, for example:

   const GOOGLE_CALENDAR_BOOKING_URL =
     "https://calendar.google.com/calendar/appointments/schedules/EXAMPLE?gv=true";

11. Save script.js and refresh booking.html.
12. Test the page on desktop and mobile before publishing.

PUBLISHING THE UPDATE
---------------------
Upload all updated files to the same GitHub repository, including the new
booking.html file. Replace the older index.html, styles.css, script.js,
START-HERE.txt, and README.md. Keep the assets folder.
