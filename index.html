About Us

Commercial cleaning that works around your schedule

Swipe Right Cleaning LLC provides dependable commercial and office cleaning for businesses throughout Plano and surrounding North Dallas communities. We specialize in after-hours cleaning so your workplace can be maintained without disrupting employees, customers, meetings, or normal operations.

Each cleaning plan is customized based on the facility’s size, condition, cleaning frequency, and requested services. Our goal is to help businesses maintain a clean, professional, and welcoming environment.

After-Hours Cleaning

Cleaning that does not interrupt your workday

We specialize in evening and after-hours commercial cleaning. Services can be scheduled after employees and customers leave, helping reduce interruptions to daily operations.

Weekend appointments may also be available depending on the facility, access requirements, and service schedule.

Some one-time projects, move-in or move-out cleanings, walkthroughs, and select services may be scheduled earlier in the day when availability allows.

Cleaning availability Primarily evenings and weekends, with limited daytime availability for select services by appointment
