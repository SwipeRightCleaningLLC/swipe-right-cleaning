About Us

Commercial cleaning that works around your schedule

Swipe Right Cleaning LLC provides dependable commercial and office cleaning for businesses throughout Plano and surrounding North Dallas communities. We specialize in after-hours cleaning so your workplace can be maintained without disrupting employees, customers, meetings, or normal operations.

Each cleaning plan is customized based on the facility’s size, condition, cleaning frequency, and requested services. Our goal is to help businesses maintain a clean, professional, and welcoming environment.

Recurring Office Cleaning

Reliable ongoing cleaning customized around your workplace and schedule.

  • Emptying trash and replacing liners
  • Dusting accessible surfaces
  • Vacuuming carpets
  • Sweeping and mopping hard floors
  • Cleaning and disinfecting restrooms
  • Cleaning breakrooms and kitchen areas
  • Wiping desks when surfaces are clear
  • Cleaning entrance doors and glass
  • Cleaning high-touch areas
  • Monitoring and restocking customer-provided restroom and breakroom supplies

Available schedules: Daily, multiple visits per week, weekly, or biweekly.

One-Time Deep Cleaning

Detailed cleaning for spaces that need extra attention or preparation before recurring service.

  • Detailed dusting of accessible surfaces
  • Baseboard cleaning
  • Heavy restroom cleaning
  • Breakroom and kitchen detailing
  • Cleaning doors, handles, and light switches
  • Removing buildup from accessible surfaces
  • Detailed vacuuming, sweeping, and mopping
  • Cleaning entrance doors and glass

Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning

Detailed cleaning before moving in, moving out, opening, or turning over a commercial space.

  • Cleaning empty offices and work areas
  • Cleaning inside cabinets and drawers
  • Cleaning restrooms and breakrooms
  • Dusting accessible surfaces
  • Cleaning doors and entrance glass
  • Cleaning floors and baseboards
  • Removing light dust and cleaning-related debris
  • Preparing the space for employees, customers, or the next tenant
