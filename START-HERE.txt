==============================================

1. PREVIEW THE WEBSITE
----------------------
Open index.html to preview the home page.
Open booking.html to preview the new inspection-booking page.

For the best local preview, open a terminal in this folder and run:

  python -m http.server 8000

Then visit:

  http://localhost:8000

2. QUOTE FORM UPDATE
--------------------
The quote form now asks whether the customer knows the exact square footage.

- If the customer selects Yes, an exact square-footage field appears.
- If the customer selects No or Not sure, an estimated range appears.
- The prepared quote email clearly labels the number as exact or estimated.

The form opens the customer's email app with the completed information addressed
to quotes@swiperightcleaningllc.com. Confirm that this email address receives mail
before publishing it publicly.
